# How to Use Instagram Outreach CRM

Welcome to the CRM! This system is designed to keep your outreach pipeline extremely fast, organized, and impossible to forget.

## Core Flow
1. **Quick Add**: Press `/` anywhere on the dashboard to immediately jump to the Quick Add input. Type the Instagram handle (without `@`) and press `Enter`.
2. **Action Priority**: Always click the colored button on a lead. It represents the *next logical step*. 
   - *Example: A "New Lead" will have a solid purple "Messaged" button. Once clicked, it will turn into an orange "Replied" button.*
3. **Follow-ups**: When you engage with a lead but don't get a definitive outcome, click `Followed Up (+2d)` to snooze them for 2 days.

## 🚨 CRITICAL RULES FOR SETTERS 🚨

- **Rule #1**: Only add leads that have *replied* or shown some engagement, unless told otherwise. Don't dump raw lists into the CRM.
- **Rule #2**: **Always set a follow-up**. If a lead is not dead, they need a follow-up date. No lead should be left floating in the "All Leads" section without a next step.
- **Rule #3**: **Clear the Overdue daily.** The red `OVERDUE FOLLOW-UPS` section at the top of your dashboard is your highest priority. Do not leave for the day if there are leads in that section.
- **Rule #4**: Update the status *immediately*. If you book a call, click "Book Call" and log it right then. Don't wait until the end of the day.

## For Admins:
- Use the **Admin Dashboard** to see daily metrics and setter activity feeds.
- Go to **Manage Users** to create new Setters. (Temp passwords will be generated for you to securely share with them).
- If a lead gets stuck, use the **Override** feature in "All Leads" to manually change their status or reassign them to a different setter.
